Confidential Transmittal Sheet Generator

Generate a real, print-ready NDA cover sheet for a sensitive document in under a minute — 100% offline, nothing leaves your browser, no account required.

What it does

  • Real transmittal sheet, not a mockup — confidentiality banner, sender/recipient blocks, an auto-generated document control number, and a handling-instructions clause (standard NDA, attorney-client privilege, or data-room-only).
  • Live preview — updates as you type, rendered entirely in your browser.
  • Export PNG or print-ready PDF — one click, no watermark negotiation, no account wall.
  • Zero upload — client names, project codenames, and NDA clauses never leave your machine. There is no server to leak from.

Who this is for

Consultants, auditors, and advisory firms who attach a formal cover sheet to sensitive documents before sending them to a client, auditor, or counterparty — and who can't run that step through a generic cloud template tool that asks for an account and stores the client's name on someone else's server.
